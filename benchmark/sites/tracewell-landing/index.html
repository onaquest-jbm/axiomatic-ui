FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does Tracewell monitor?

Tracewell tracks request volume, latency, errors, and distributed traces for instrumented APIs. You can look at an individual request or compare performance across endpoints.

Can I use my existing OpenTelemetry setup?

Yes. You can export traces from an existing OpenTelemetry Collector. The Node.js quickstart shows an SDK-based setup for a service you’re instrumenting for the first time.

What request data do I need to send?

Route, timing, and status are enough to get started. You can configure your SDK or collector to drop sensitive headers and attributes before export.

How long can I look back at traces?

Starter keeps traces for 7 days and Team keeps them for 30 days. Scale offers configurable retention.
